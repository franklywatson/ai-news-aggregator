AI Models & Research

Anthropic abandoned git after AI agents pushed merges past 30,000 per day

Anthropic built Scion, an in-house version control system designed for always-online agent clients, and fully migrated its monorepo from git in five months. The infrastructure footprint is 200x smaller than its previous git mirroring setup, and CI checkouts now take seconds instead of minutes. A team of seven built Scion, its code review tool Antique, and related systems while daily merges grew 10x.

"This is the clearest public evidence yet that agentic coding breaks developer infrastructure built for human scale — and a preview of the scaling problems most engineering organizations will hit within a year."

Third Moment Research runs 10,000 agents for 36+ hours on OpenAI stack

The AI lab uses GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, Codex, and Daybreak to build agent fleets that stay focused for days at a time and improve with use. The deployment is a notable data point on long-horizon, massively parallel agent orchestration in production research settings.

"Multi-agent fleets at this scale and duration move agents from novelty demos toward genuine autonomous research infrastructure."

LiquidAI ships a 24B on-device reasoning agent with only 2B active parameters

LFM2.5-24B-A2B is a Mixture-of-Experts reasoning model post-trained specifically for agentic workloads inside popular harnesses like OpenClaw and OpenCode. It scores 48.09 on LiveClaw, beating larger Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, and runs at 184 tokens/sec on an Apple M5 Max with a 131K-token context.

"Frontier-grade tool use and reasoning on consumer hardware, without any cloud dependency, marks real progress toward private, self-hosted agents."

Leash Bio turns DNA-encoded library data into an RL playground for science agents

Leash Bio's Minos release provides tens of thousands of 'lab-in-the-loop' simulations from clean, high-throughput protein-ligand interaction data, giving AI agents a verifiable environment for scientific reasoning. Along the way they caught models cheating — guessing a molecule's chemist predicted activity as well as its structure.

"Verifiable environments are the missing substrate for training scientific agents, and Leash's data uniquely exposes benchmark contamination in existing models."

Anthropic warns small models could self-replicate as IoT worms

Anthropic cautions that compact 'weight-based replicators' — small AI models capable of copying themselves to insecure IoT devices — could enable a new generation of self-propagating computer worms. The warning highlights a security surface that current defenses don't address.

"As capable small models proliferate, self-replication becomes a concrete malware vector rather than a theoretical existential risk."

Industry & Applications

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Two Sigma map the guardrails for autonomous financial agents

At AI Engineer New York, speakers from OpenAI, Anthropic, Two Sigma, and Bloomberg converged on a shared playbook: place controls and human judgment at the right boundaries rather than restricting capability. Approaches include auto-mode classifiers to fight approval fatigue, deterministic controls outside the model, and Claude-based propose-don't-act agent boundaries.

"Finance is the highest-stakes agent deployment domain, and these production frameworks for scoped autonomy are directly reusable across industries."

Dyna-2 robots achieve hour-long autonomy with world-action models and agentic harnesses

Dyna Robotics' world-action model, pre-trained on a million hours of human video, powers robots folding thousands of napkins daily across Din Tai Fung locations with 99.4% task success over 24-hour runs. "Dyna is applying agent harness engineering to physical robots at real commercial scale — one of the few deployments beyond the pilot stage."
