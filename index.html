Sakana AI Unveils Differentiable Multi-Agent Coordination Framework

In testing, this multi-agent architecture solved complex Sudoku puzzles with a 93% success rate, vastly outperforming standard message-passing baselines.

Tencent Releases Hy3: A 295B Parameter Open-Weights MoE Model

Tencent has launched Hy3, a new Apache 2.0 licensed Mixture-of-Experts model featuring 295 billion total parameters with 21 billion active per token. The model supports a 256,000-token context window and is currently available for free via OpenRouter for testing.

"Hy3 represents another highly competitive, open-weights architecture coming out of China that rivals Western flagship models, specifically highlighting significant utility gains for agentic and productivity tasks."
