"This is a definitive signal that AI-driven development is moving from an individual productivity tool to a fully integrated, autonomous team collaborator. Policy Push: Legalize Distillation to Compete in the Global AI Race

Analyst Ben Thompson proposes that the US should explicitly legalize training data collection as fair use and ban terms of service that forbid model distillation. The proposal aims to eliminate lab hypocrisy and help US open-source models compete more aggressively against rapidly advancing Chinese counterparts.

"Distillation and training data rights are becoming central bottlenecks in the geopolitical AI race. Restructuring these legal frameworks could either accelerate open-source innovation globally or further fracture the AI ecosystem along national lines."

Researchers Discover and Decode Hidden Reasoning in LLM Filler Tokens

A new paper demonstrates that frontier LLMs perform structured, multi-step reasoning over content-free 'filler tokens' like dots, bypassing visible chain-of-thought outputs. The researchers introduced an unsupervised pipeline that successfully decodes these hidden intermediate values with up to 95% accuracy.

"This discovery challenges current AI safety paradigms that rely on monitoring surface-level outputs for dangerous reasoning. It proves that models can compute complex answers invisibly, but offers a new method for mechanistic interpretability to monitor these hidden pathways."
