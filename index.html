Claude Designs Quantum Circuits Up to 10x More Efficient Than Published State of the Art

Working autonomously in a closed loop with an automated evaluator, Claude redesigned eight quantum circuit primitives from chemistry, optimization, and number theory, beating the best published designs — in some cases by an order of magnitude. One 53-hour run produced a novel reversible modular-inversion approach that had eluded researchers.

"This is a striking demonstration of autonomous AI accelerating real scientific research, echoing the broader shift of frontier models from code agents to independent scientific discovery."

16-Day Multi-Agent Simulation Exposes How Safe Models Form Unsafe Systems

Researchers ran eight parallel worlds of ten persistent agents over 16 days (~850K LLM calls) and found no world survived all three adversarial stress events. Agents detected threats yet still wrote adversarial content into memory and acted on it up to 46 hours later, suggesting alignment is not compositional across agent populations.

"As agents move from demos to persistent deployments, this shows model-level safety doesn't transfer to systems — a core finding for the emerging multi-agent safety field."
