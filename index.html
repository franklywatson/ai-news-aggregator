AI Models & Research

Google ships Gemini 4 Carbon, its frontier model for long-horizon agentic work

Carbon leads independent agentic benchmarks including CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR, and Zapier's AutomationBench at more than 2x lower cost per task than the next best external model. Google is also previewing /swarm in Antigravity, turning a single prompt into a coordinated multi-agent team with adversarial verifiers, and reports strong results in cybersecurity, formal verification, and scientific research.

"Carbon is explicitly built for multi-step agentic workflows, and the /swarm orchestration preview signals that multi-agent coordination is becoming a first-class product feature rather than a research demo."

Gemini 4 Hydrogen becomes the default model powering Google Search's AI Mode

Google's lowest-cost Gemini 4 model is now the default in AI Mode and handles 'the hardest questions' in AI Overviews, breaking multi-part and ambiguous queries into subtopics and adding richer visual outputs. It is also the new free tier model across Gemini apps and AI Studio.

"Deploying a frontier-family model at Search scale is one of the largest agent-adjacent production rollouts anywhere, and sets the economics baseline the rest of the industry will be compared against."

Google launches Gemini Decision 1, a $0.10-per-million single-pass decision model

Google beat expectations by shipping a production calibrated decision model that scores closed-set choices in a single pass with ~200ms latency and up to 1,000 questions per request. Cloudflare demonstrated using such fast decision models for tool selection and log triage, cutting six LLM calls down to one and 40K tokens to 1.8K.

"Dedicated decision models radically change agent architecture economics — offloading routing, classification, and tool selection from expensive reasoning models is the hybrid pattern most production agent teams are converging on."

Deleting 0.05% of weights matches full fine-tuning, paper shows

Deletion Tuning learns a binary mask that zeroes fewer than 0.05% of gate projection weights, matching full fine-tuning across RL, SFT, and distillation while forgetting far less. The resulting masks are hundreds to thousands of times smaller than fine-tuned checkpoints.

"If post-training mostly selects latent capabilities rather than adding them, this reshapes how teams think about cheap model customization and continual learning for specialized agents."

Gemini Robotics 2.5 hits 96% success sorting car parts on live Mercedes lines

DeepMind's latest vision-language-action model achieved a 96% pick-and-pack success rate with Apptronik's Apollo 2 humanoid on active Mercedes-Benz production lines, with 30-100% higher success rates across 200 tasks. Gemini 4 Argon served as a simulation engine to accelerate training, and the release includes a new open robotics jailbreak benchmark.

"This is a rare agent deployment measured on a real factory floor, and using frontier models as simulation engines for training physical agents points at a broader pattern of models bootstrapping other models."

Developer Tools & Infrastructure

GitHub's practical guide: build agent skills once, run them on any agent

GitHub lays out how to write portable SKILL.md-based agent skills that work across Claude Code, Cursor, and Copilot, with patterns for shared manifests, adversarial verification, and human approval checkpoints. The guide emphasizes separating instructions from environment: skills describe how to do work, while hosts provide tools, credentials, and permissions.

"Agent skills are emerging as the portable unit of reusable engineering knowledge, and vendor-neutral authoring patterns are what will make multi-agent toolchains maintainable rather than siloed."

Why a pile of MCP connectors is not a context engine

Unblocked argues that MCP connectors give agents access to sources but leave cross-source assembly, contradiction resolution, and institutional knowledge to be reconstructed at inference time on your token bill. Their benchmarks show pre-resolved context finishing tasks up to 83% faster with roughly 40% fewer tokens, and they open-sourced a simulator to reproduce the results on your own repo.

"As MCP adoption explodes, the next bottleneck is context engineering — doing reconciliation and synthesis at ingestion time rather than burning frontier-model tokens on search loops."

A walkthrough of what it takes to run a 'software factory' from ticket to pull request, treating the loops around agents — verification, review, and feedback — as the core engineering problem rather than the model itself.

"Harness engineering is becoming a recognized discipline; teams that invest in the scaffolding around agents are consistently outperforming those relying on raw model capability."

No, AI agents did not discover an unknown Maya site — it was published last year

A Google researcher's widely shared claim that 48 AI agents found an unmapped Maya site fell apart when archaeologists showed it was documented in a journal over a year earlier. The episode highlights how agent-driven 'discoveries' can skip the literature review that real science requires and shift verification burdens onto domain experts.

"A sharp cautionary tale as autonomous research agents proliferate: agents that don't engage with existing literature produce confident, wrong claims — and expert communities are already being flooded by them."

ArtCraft is using AI agents to open-source the entire software ecosystem

The startup has shipped 17 open-source AI-built apps, including a Rust-based Photoshop clone at roughly 60-76% parity, downloading at 1.7 million times per day with ~4,000 community developers. Founder Brandon Thomas's team lets agents handle immutable desktop apps while humans own schema migrations and API design, and plans to open-source 'AutoCraft,' a fully autonomous software factory.

"It's the most aggressive proof yet that agents can build production-quality software at scale, and its human/agent division of labor is a template other teams are watching closely."

Secure GitHub Access for AI Agents: Skip Classic Tokens

Kent C. Dodds recommends using fine-grained GitHub tokens for AI agents instead of classic tokens, which grant broad write access. Use read-only scopes on only necessary repos with a 90-day expiry.

"As developers increasingly hand repo access to AI coding agents, least-privilege token setup is a critical and often-overlooked security practice that can prevent accidental or malicious writes."
