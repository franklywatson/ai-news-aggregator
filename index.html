AI Models & Research

Google Launches Gemini Decisions API, Bringing Multimodal 'Decision Models' to the Mainstream

Google's new Gemini Decision 1 model scores bounded choices (yes/no, multiple choice, rubric grading) in a single forward pass across text, images, audio, video, and PDFs, returning probabilities in 150-250ms at $0.10 per million input tokens. Google internally uses it as a low-latency control plane for model routing in Gemini Notebook, safety filtering for MCP tools in Search, and instant UI interactions in Stitch.

"This cements 'decision models' as a distinct model category alongside generative LLMs, with all three frontier providers now shipping purpose-built APIs. For agent builders, these cheap, calibrated primitives are ideal for routing, gating, and context-grading inside agent harnesses."

Weight-Edited Qwen 27B Thinks Less and Scores Higher — No Training Required

Researchers released Qwen Concept 27B, a Qwen3.8-27B variant with nine down-projection matrices rewritten via 'weight-space programming' derived purely from the model's own activations. Against the base model it gains +4.3 on GPQA Diamond and +13 on LiveCodeBench while producing 27-54% fewer thinking tokens, with norm-matched random edits showing no effect as a control.

"Direct weight-space intervention improving reasoning efficiency without gradients or data hints at a new, cheap post-training lever — especially relevant for reducing cost and latency in agentic loops."

First Rigorous Router Benchmark: No Model Router Beats the Frontier on Cost and Quality

Vals AI evaluated six model routers (OpenRouter, Fireworks Nexus, Ramp, TypeSafe's Jev Router, Sakana's Fugu Max) on ~1,250 agentic tasks and found none on the cost-accuracy frontier — each was beaten on both dimensions by at least one single model, with cheaper models like DeepSeek V4.1 Flash often dominating. The analysis also reveals routers silently lowering reasoning effort, hurting long coding tasks.

"Model routing is a core pillar of multi-model agent orchestration, and this is among the first independent, task-level evaluations. The finding that routing leaves 8-27 points on the table suggests big headroom for better orchestration — and caution for buyers."
