"The first flagship release since Anthropic's call to 'pace the frontier' pairs aggressive price cuts with safety claims — a direct challenge to GPT-6 Astra on cost-per-task for agentic coding and long-horizon work."

Stanford: self-organizing agent teams beat their best member by 18 points

Self-Organizing Agent Teams (SAT) learn reusable teamwork strategies — roles, conversational phases, and information flow — from just 15-25 training problems, then transfer them unchanged to unseen benchmarks. Teams averaged 66.7% accuracy across math and physics benchmarks versus 48.8% for the strongest solo agent and 59.0% for a perfect router.

"This shows organization itself can be a learned agent capability, a significant step beyond fixed multi-agent orchestration protocols toward emergent coordination."

Together AI shows how to fine-tune your own Jev-style classifier for $17

Together released Tev1-4B-experimental, a Jev-like classification model built on Qwen3.5 4B, along with a full tutorial for fine-tuning a custom version for about $17 and 25 minutes of training. The approach turns small LLMs into fast, cheap state-evaluation components for pipelines like return approvals, intent detection, and policy checks.

"Cheap specialized classifier models are becoming standard building blocks for agent harnesses, letting developers route and gate agentic workflows without paying frontier-model prices."

CrisperWhisper 2.0: open verbatim speech-to-text with 30ms word timing

CrisperWhisper 2.0 adds a controllable switch between verbatim transcripts (every filler, stutter, and vocal sound) and clean 'intended' output, with 30ms word-level timing error and multilingual support. Its Verbatimize technique can retroactively upgrade existing clean speech corpora into verbatim-annotated ones.

"Verbatim disfluency data is the raw material for natural speech synthesis and clinical analysis; making it cheaply scalable opens new speech-agent applications."
