AI Models & Research

OpenAI's 10,000-Agent Swarm Solves Navier-Stokes in 88 Hours, Reshaping Frontier Research

OpenAI reports that roughly 10,000 agents, trained for a year via multi-agent RL and run on massive parallel test-time compute, produced a claimed solution to the Navier-Stokes Millennium Problem in 88 hours at a cost exceeding $40M. The result signals a shift from scaling single models toward self-organizing agent ensembles as the new frontier paradigm. Mathematical community verification is still pending.

"This is the clearest evidence yet that massive multi-agent coordination at inference time — not just bigger models — can crack problems at the very frontier of human knowledge."

Second Mathematician Accuses OpenAI of 'Dishonesty' Over Training Data Transparency

Mathematician Andreas Thom says OpenAI refused to clarify whether his ChatGPT interactions entered training data that may have contributed to its non-sofic groups result, calling the company's answers 'materially misleading.' OpenAI can deny direct data access but won't rule out indirect influence from de-identified user data. Researchers fear the episode could push mathematics toward secrecy.

"The provenance of user conversations in frontier training pipelines is now a live trust crisis between AI labs and the research communities they depend on — with direct implications for anyone using Codex or ChatGPT on unpublished work."

Anthropic Models Three Scenarios for How AI Reshapes US Jobs and Wages by 2030

Anthropic's Economics team released an interactive scenario explorer modeling how AI capability and adoption levels translate into GDP growth, unemployment, and wage outcomes through 2030. In the extreme scenario, GDP grows ~15% annually but knowledge-work unemployment rises beyond recessionary levels and the labor share of income falls. A survey of 10,000+ Americans found most expectations align with the 'substantial change' scenario.

"This is one of the most rigorous public frameworks connecting AI capability assumptions to concrete labor-market outcomes, and will inform policy debates on managing the transition."

DeepMind's AlphaGenome Atlas Predicts the Impact of All 9 Billion Possible DNA Mutations

Google DeepMind precomputed AlphaGenome predictions for every possible single-letter DNA variant in the human genome, releasing a 1-petabyte atlas with a unified Variant Impact score. Collaborators have already used it to solve previously undiagnosed rare disease cases and uncover non-coding variant associations in UK Biobank data.

"A precomputed map of the entire mutation space turns months of per-variant analysis into a lookup, and is already producing experimentally validated discoveries."

The 'MIGHT Stack': A Composable Architecture for Agentic Development with Open Models

Together.ai lays out a five-layer stack — Model, Inference, Gateways/Routers, Harness, and Tools (Skills and MCP) — showing how keeping each layer independent lets developers swap in new open models in minutes. "As open models close the quality gap, this is becoming the canonical mental model for building swappable, provider-agnostic agent stacks instead of locking into a single vendor's harness."

AI Built a Zero-Click WeChat Worm in Days, Demonstrating AI's Offensive Security Power

Calif Research demoed WeWorm, the first zero-click worm spreading through WeChat calls on iOS and Android, with AI doing most of the work: finding the bug and writing the RCE exploit took about two days, and the worm one more week. The team notes this class of capability previously took larger teams months.

"This is a concrete datapoint that AI has collapsed the cost and timeline of building top-tier offensive capabilities — reinforcing the urgent need for AI-assisted defense and faster patch cycles."

US Agencies Name Six Chinese Firms in Industrial-Scale Frontier Model Distillation

The NSA, CISA, and FBI accused DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Alibaba, MiniMax, StepFun, and Z.AI of industrial-scale distillation attacks against US frontier models since late 2024, allegedly with Chinese government awareness. "The allegations will likely reshape API access policies, geo-restrictions, and chain-of-thought exposure across all frontier providers — directly affecting legitimate developers using these models."
