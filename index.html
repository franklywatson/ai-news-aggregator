Datadog Deleted Its AI Context Files—and It Actually Worked

Datadog's Language Foundations team deleted an entire folder of carefully maintained AI context files and found that performance improved, challenging the assumption that more structured context is always better for agent workflows. The finding suggests that managing conversational residue and reducing context noise may be more effective than providing extensive pre-written guidance.

"This provides striking real-world validation for minimal context engineering, implying that 'less is more' when structuring agent environments, with direct implications for cost reduction and harness design."

EU AI Act Transparency Rules Now in Effect: Chatbots and Deepfakes Must Be Labeled

The European Union's landmark AI Act transparency obligations took effect August 2nd, requiring companies to disclose when users are interacting with AI and to label AI-generated or manipulated audio, image, and video content. Companies face fines up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover for non-compliance, with existing services given until December to comply.

"This establishes the first comprehensive, enforceable AI labeling regime at scale, fundamentally changing how AI products must surface their capabilities to European users."
