AI Models & Research

Simon Willison: Three LLM inflection points in twelve months — and a fourth may be underway

Willison traces coding agents from Claude Opus 4.5's first reliably-correct results, through 'Fable class' goal-driven agents, to frontier models that suddenly excel at 3D modeling and even game design. He speculates Claude Fable 5.2 may be a fourth inflection point: describe software clearly and it gets built.

"A first-hand practitioner account of the trajectory from 'sometimes useful assistant' to 'describe it and it ships' — arguably the clearest framing of how fast agent capabilities have compounded."

GPT-6.2 Astra identifies creator of anonymous 1808 sketchbook in 22 minutes

The professor predicts a milder 'Deep Blue moment' for historians than the one mathematics is facing.

"A concrete, verified case of an agentic system outperforming human researchers in archival scholarship — evidence that autonomous research impact is spreading beyond math and code."

OpenAI publishes five years of pretraining loss curves and proposes shared development limits

Chief scientist Jakub Pachocki disclosed log-perplexity figures back to GPT-3.5, arguing capabilities jumped when losses dropped below 0.8 and projecting transformative capabilities around 0.68–0.70. Microsoft's Mustafa Suleyman cosigned and reported MAI-2 Base at 0.819, the first lab to join the proposed self-regulatory limit.

"A first-of-its-kind transparency move converting formerly 'sensitive IP' into a shared pacing metric — but critics note tokenizer bias, post-training compute, and synthetic data confounds could make the metric misleading."

DeepMind's Gemini agents discover formally verified quantum programs up to 290x cheaper

Gemini 4 agent teams synthesized novel quantum programs against Lean-verified specifications, achieving order-of-magnitude cost reductions for fusion, photochemistry, and drug-metabolism simulations. The agents collaborated via wikis and organized 'conferences' to share results.

"Demonstrates multi-agent discovery loops that produce provably correct algorithms beyond what classical computers can directly test — a template for AI-driven science under mathematical rigor."

Z.ai's GLM-5.4 loops transformer layers to punch above its parameter count

GLM-5.4 converts a pretrained 750B base into a loop transformer that runs 28 middle layers twice, claiming 2T–3T-class reasoning performance with vision, targeting games, shaders, and CAD. Open weights are promised after 'safety hardening,' though no benchmarks have been published yet.

"Loop architectures could deliver frontier-class reasoning at a fraction of the parameters, but the delayed, caveat-laden open-weight release is drawing skepticism about nerfed capabilities."

Developer Tools & Infrastructure

Claude Ship: Anthropic automates the entire pull request lifecycle

Claude Ship combines Code Review, a new PR Steward agent that fixes failing CI and review feedback, and Claude Approvals, which enforces repo-defined policies like a smarter CODEOWNERS file. Developers can trigger it with a single /ship command in Claude Code, and Anthropic reports merged PRs more than doubled on its own team while revert rates fell 40%.

"This extends agentic coding from writing code to reviewing, fixing, and approving it — moving policy-based autonomy directly into the merge pipeline, with clear human-oversight controls."

Anthropic launches Claude plugins with 10,000+ tools across all plans

Claude now supports a plugin ecosystem exposing over 10,000 tools to all plan tiers, dramatically expanding what the assistant can do out of the box. The move positions Claude as an extensible agent platform rather than a closed chatbot.

"A broad tool ecosystem is a prerequisite for general-purpose agents; making it available on all plans lowers the barrier to agentic workflows for everyday users."

OpenAI and Cisco release Shirudo, agents that patch live Linux systems with eBPF shields

Shirudo is an open-source Linux runtime in which agents write dynamic eBPF-based defenses that block exploits without downtime. It represents a defensive counterpoint to mounting evidence of agents autonomously exploiting vulnerabilities, including one that took a medical-imaging zero-day in ~11 minutes.

"Agent-driven offensive capability is now demonstrated; Shirudo is one of the first concrete open-source answers using the same agentic techniques for defense."

Industry & Applications

AI agents are overwhelming America's public records systems

Venture-backed startups are filing hundreds of thousands of FOIA requests to build AI-powered government sales signals, while citizens use chatbots to file longer, broader requests — federal agencies received a record 1.7 million last year. States are responding with laws that let agencies deny suspected AI or bulk requests, raising transparency concerns.

"An early, concrete case of agentic and AI-assisted activity stressing civic infrastructure designed for human-scale volumes — and of regulation riskily reacting to agents rather than to bad actors."

high

Vibe-coding agents force music gear makers to go open source

AI coding agents and tools like Reverse Engineer Anything have made every music device hackable, and manufacturers are split: Akai's parent inMusic and Behringer are embracing open firmware and SDKs, while Elektron bans custom-firmware links from its forums. A $70 hackable DX7 clone with a dozen community firmwares kicked off the movement.

"A striking second-order effect of agentic coding: when any device's firmware can be reverse-engineered and rewritten by AI, 'closed' becomes untenable and open source becomes a competitive strategy."

medium

Tidal will delete 29 million AI-generated songs in platform-wide ban

Tidal is banning fully AI-generated music on December 9th, deleting roughly 29 million tracks and rejecting 80,000–85,000 per day after AI music hit 43% of new distributions. The platform cites data showing 96% of AI songs get zero plays, and will use detection tooling while permitting AI-assisted work.

"The strongest line yet drawn by a streaming platform against AI slop, setting a policy template that distinguishes AI-assisted from AI-generated content — a distinction the whole content industry is wrestling with."

Claude reverse-engineers a Panasonic AC bus protocol for Home Assistant control

A hobbyist used Claude to decode the 2-wire R1/R2 protocol on Panasonic ducted air conditioners, producing a complete open-source ESP32 interface with ESPHome integration, protocol docs, and PCB designs. The writeup details how the agent mapped the ~19 kHz carrier, frame format, and register layout.

"A small but vivid example of frontier models autonomously doing the kind of protocol reverse-engineering that previously required specialist expertise — the same dynamic now roiling the music gear industry."

Agent Guardrails: Why System Prompts Aren't Enough

Kent C. Dodds shares a mental model for AI agent safety: actions should be sorted by who must be present, then enforced outside the model. Prompt instructions are polite requests; real guardrails can't be talked past.

"As coding agents gain autonomy, teams need enforced guardrails rather than prompt-level requests, making this a practical framework for anyone deploying agents."
